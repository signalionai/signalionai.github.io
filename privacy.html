RadinTube privacy policy

RadinTube is a private video library used by a single family. It is not offered to the public.

Google Drive access

RadinTube asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, RadinTube can see and change only the files it created itself (its own "RadinTubeFiles" folder). It cannot see any other file in the Google account.

RadinTube stores the family's videos, thumbnails and backups in that folder. It does not read, collect, sell or share any other Google user data.

Where data goes

Files in RadinTubeFiles are used only by the family's own server and devices, to play and back up the family's videos. Nothing is sent to advertisers or other third parties.

Removing access

The account owner can remove RadinTube's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the RadinTubeFiles folder in Google Drive.
