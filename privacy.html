Privacy policy

This private tool is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Google Drive access

The tool asks only for Google's drive.file permission. With it, the tool can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Google account.

It does not collect, sell or share any user data, and nothing is sent to advertisers or other third parties.

Removing access

The account owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
